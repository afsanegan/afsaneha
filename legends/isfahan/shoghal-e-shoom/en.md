Preserved across centuries within the rich oral folklore of **Isfahan** (encircling the historical domains of **اصفهان / کاشان**), the legendary lore of **Ominous Jackal** stands as a compelling testament to Iran's enduring mythic imagination. Transmitted across hearths, mountain paths, and rural caravans, the tale has survived through the living memories of local elders.

Folklorists generally categorize **Ominous Jackal** within the mythological taxonomy of *موجود افسانه‌ای*. As with many traditional entities of Iranian folk belief, it embodies both the untamed natural elements and the ancestral anxieties regarding solitary wilderness. Classical collectors recorded its traditional traits as follows:

> "Illustration by Erfan Aghvami According to people living in Barzrood village (Located in Isfahan province, Central Iran), there's a creature called 'Ominous Jackal' or 'Sagorg'. Ominous Jackal is a combination of a dog and a wolf. It has a terrifying voice that is like somebody shouting with a very thin voice. It is believed that if this jackal shouts at anyone's door, someone in that house will die.“About sixteen years ago, the sound of this creature was heard from behind the house of the head of the village and his father died two days after it.” - Reports a local, Bijan Kherad Translation: Sarah Mohebbi Moghadam …"

In local oral tradition, an encounter with **Ominous Jackal** was regarded as a formidable threshold between familiar domestic reality and the unseen realm. Elders customarily advised night travelers, shepherds, and caravan drivers traversing the historic passes of Isfahan to heed ancient apotropaic rituals—such as carrying iron amulets, maintaining solemn reverence towards nature, and never uttering provocative words—to pass through its territorial domain unharmed.

Today, the chronicle of **Ominous Jackal** forms an invaluable component of Iran's intangible cultural heritage, offering modern readers an evocative window into how ancient communities interpreted their ecology, mystery, and moral balance.
