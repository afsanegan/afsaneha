Preserved across centuries within the rich oral folklore of **Gilan** (encircling the historical domains of **رشت / لاهیجان**), the legendary lore of **ĀDAM ĀBEE** stands as a compelling testament to Iran's enduring mythic imagination. Transmitted across hearths, mountain paths, and rural caravans, the tale has survived through the living memories of local elders.

Folklorists generally categorize **ĀDAM ĀBEE** within the mythological taxonomy of *موجود افسانه‌ای*. As with many traditional entities of Iranian folk belief, it embodies both the untamed natural elements and the ancestral anxieties regarding solitary wilderness. Classical collectors recorded its traditional traits as follows:

> "Illustration by Farima Keshavarz In the beliefs of the people of Dezful, there exists a being known as "Adam-e Abi" or "Oovi Adam." The "Water People" live in the waters of rivers, especially in the Dez River. From the waist down, they resemble fish, and from the waist up, they resemble humans. They have black skin and long, curly hair. These beings sometimes emerge from underwater and rest on rocky cliffs. The people considered these beings sacred and revered them with respect, a belief that has its roots in the past. Belief in the queen of waters or water angels existed in the religions of the past in this land. There are many myths and legends surrounding these beings. …"

In local oral tradition, an encounter with **ĀDAM ĀBEE** was regarded as a formidable threshold between familiar domestic reality and the unseen realm. Elders customarily advised night travelers, shepherds, and caravan drivers traversing the historic passes of Gilan to heed ancient apotropaic rituals—such as carrying iron amulets, maintaining solemn reverence towards nature, and never uttering provocative words—to pass through its territorial domain unharmed.

Today, the chronicle of **ĀDAM ĀBEE** forms an invaluable component of Iran's intangible cultural heritage, offering modern readers an evocative window into how ancient communities interpreted their ecology, mystery, and moral balance.
