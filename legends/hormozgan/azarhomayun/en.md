Preserved across centuries within the rich oral folklore of **Hormozgan** (encircling the historical domains of **قشم / بندرعباس**), the legendary lore of **Azarhomayun** stands as a compelling testament to Iran's enduring mythic imagination. Transmitted across hearths, mountain paths, and rural caravans, the tale has survived through the living memories of local elders.

Folklorists generally categorize **Azarhomayun** within the mythological taxonomy of *موجود افسانه‌ای*. As with many traditional entities of Iranian folk belief, it embodies both the untamed natural elements and the ancestral anxieties regarding solitary wilderness. Classical collectors recorded its traditional traits as follows:

> "Illustration by Farima Keshavarz Azarhomayoun is the name of a witch from Nezami’s Eskandar-Nama, who was a descendant of Sam. She turned herself into a dragon and stopped Eskandar when he was about to destroy the fire temple of Isfahan (Central Iran). When his plan failed, Eskandar asked Bolinas, the Greek sage, to kill the dragon. He managed to nullify Azarhomayoun’s magic. After she was turned back into herself, Eskandar captured her and gave her to Bolinas.Bolinas married Azarhomayoun. It is said that people called him [Bolinas, the Magician] because he learned how to do magic from her. Translation: Sarah Mohebbi Moghadam …"

In local oral tradition, an encounter with **Azarhomayun** was regarded as a formidable threshold between familiar domestic reality and the unseen realm. Elders customarily advised night travelers, shepherds, and caravan drivers traversing the historic passes of Hormozgan to heed ancient apotropaic rituals—such as carrying iron amulets, maintaining solemn reverence towards nature, and never uttering provocative words—to pass through its territorial domain unharmed.

Today, the chronicle of **Azarhomayun** forms an invaluable component of Iran's intangible cultural heritage, offering modern readers an evocative window into how ancient communities interpreted their ecology, mystery, and moral balance.
