Preserved across centuries within the rich oral folklore of **Golestan** (encircling the historical domains of **گرگان**), the legendary lore of **The widow ghoul** stands as a compelling testament to Iran's enduring mythic imagination. Transmitted across hearths, mountain paths, and rural caravans, the tale has survived through the living memories of local elders.

Folklorists generally categorize **The widow ghoul** within the mythological taxonomy of *Div*. As with many traditional entities of Iranian folk belief, it embodies both the untamed natural elements and the ancestral anxieties regarding solitary wilderness. Classical collectors recorded its traditional traits as follows:

> "Illustration by Mo.Rasoulipour According to the people living in Duzin village (located in Golestan province, northern Iran), there's a ghoul called [Biveh Ghoul]. This ghoul's body is covered with hair. Biveh Ghoul enjoys imitating people and whatever they would do, like working on farms or cooking.One day Mir Hasan's Mom told people who lived in the village, "we could smear oil all over our hands and legs and act like we're trying to set ourselves on fire. Biveh Ghoul will imitate us and set itself on fire.". People agreed and did what she told them, and the ghoul set itself on fire by mistake. Translation: Sarah Mohebbi Moghadam"

In local oral tradition, an encounter with **The widow ghoul** was regarded as a formidable threshold between familiar domestic reality and the unseen realm. Elders customarily advised night travelers, shepherds, and caravan drivers traversing the historic passes of Golestan to heed ancient apotropaic rituals—such as carrying iron amulets, maintaining solemn reverence towards nature, and never uttering provocative words—to pass through its territorial domain unharmed.

Today, the chronicle of **The widow ghoul** forms an invaluable component of Iran's intangible cultural heritage, offering modern readers an evocative window into how ancient communities interpreted their ecology, mystery, and moral balance.
